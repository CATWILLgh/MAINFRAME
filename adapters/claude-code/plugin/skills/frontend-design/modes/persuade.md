# Persuade

Use for landing pages, product introductions, campaigns, pricing, and conversion-oriented surfaces.

- Establish one clear promise, one primary action, and a credible evidence path.
- Order content by the visitor's questions: what it is, why it matters, proof, fit, risk, and next step.
- Use generous spacing and expressive typography deliberately, while keeping reading and navigation effortless.
- Treat trust signals, pricing conditions, limitations, and form expectations as product information, not decoration.
- Keep calls to action specific and consistent; secondary actions must not compete equally.
- Verify mobile reading order, meaningful landmarks, form recovery, performance-sensitive media, and reduced motion.

Avoid generic hero copy, repeated calls to action without new evidence, decorative dashboards, and visual novelty that obscures the offer.
